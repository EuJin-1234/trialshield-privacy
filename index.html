Privacy Policy for TrialShield

Last updated: August 15, 2026

TrialShield: Trial Reminder is designed to help users remember when free trials are ending. We take privacy seriously and have designed the app to process and store information locally on your device whenever possible.

1. Information TrialShield Processes

TrialShield processes information that you intentionally provide to the app, including screenshots, images, text, or links that you choose to share with TrialShield.

This information may contain details such as:

  • Service or subscription name
  • Free trial duration
  • Trial end date
  • Expected renewal or first-charge date
  • Subscription price
  • Currency
  • Billing period

2. Screenshot and Text Processing

When you share a screenshot with TrialShield, the app uses on-device text recognition to extract relevant trial information.

Screenshot and text recognition is performed locally on your device. TrialShield does not upload your screenshots or extracted text to our servers.

TrialShield only processes content that you explicitly choose to share with the app. The app does not continuously monitor your screen or automatically access your photo library.

3. Data Storage

Trial and reminder information is stored locally on your device so that TrialShield can display protected trials and schedule reminders.

The current version of TrialShield does not use a cloud account or remote database to store your trial information.

Deleting the app may remove locally stored TrialShield data, depending on your device and Android settings.

4. Permissions

TrialShield may request permissions that are necessary for its core functionality, such as permission to send notifications.

TrialShield does not require access to:

  • Your bank account
  • Your email inbox
  • Your SMS messages
  • Your contacts
  • Your full photo library
  • Your device screen through an Accessibility Service

5. Notifications

TrialShield uses Android notifications to remind you before a protected free trial is expected to end or renew.

Notification scheduling is performed using information stored locally on your device.

6. Internet and External Links

TrialShield may open external websites or Google Play pages when you choose to manage a subscription or follow a link.

These external websites and services are operated by third parties and are subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

7. Data Sharing

TrialShield does not sell your personal information.

The current version of TrialShield does not share your screenshots, extracted trial information, or locally stored trial records with advertisers or data brokers.

8. Advertising and Analytics

The current version of TrialShield does not contain advertising SDKs.

TrialShield does not currently use third-party behavioral analytics or advertising tracking services.

9. Accounts

TrialShield does not currently require users to create an account or provide personal information such as a name, email address, or phone number.

10. Children's Privacy

TrialShield is not specifically designed for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Data Security

TrialShield is designed to minimize data collection and keep trial information on your device.

However, no software or device can guarantee absolute security. Users should maintain appropriate security protections on their Android devices.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when TrialShield features or data practices change.

When significant changes are made, the updated policy will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
